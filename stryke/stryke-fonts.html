Display Font Candidates

Every card renders the identical specimen at the real ramp sizes — the displayTitle headline (42), the display hero band (56) with its SemiCondensed overline, and a display-voice CTA chip. Only the display family changes; overlines stay on Barlow SemiCondensed and the palette stays on #C9F53A over #07090B, so what you are judging is the face and nothing else.

Exploration only — the app still ships Anton / Black Han Sans / Barlow. Verdicts and the swap procedure live in FONTS-EXPLORATION.md beside this file.
