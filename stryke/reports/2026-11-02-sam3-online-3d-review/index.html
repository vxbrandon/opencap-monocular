Camera assumptions

Indoor clips use intrinsics supplied in their manifests. The imported football clips use the author’s image-diagonal focal heuristic and centred principal point. This body benchmark did not calibrate those cameras.

For the earlier two-view ball experiment, focal length was estimated from the scene; original lens-calibration sidecars were unavailable. The fit assumed zero lens distortion, square pixels and a centred principal point. Those choices make the result conditional. Known goal dimensions supply useful constraints, while a full camera/distortion model needs additional evidence.
