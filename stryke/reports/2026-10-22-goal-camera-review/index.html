How were the green goal corners selected?

I inspected enlarged source images and pixel-coordinate grids, identified the front crossbar/post junctions and upright bases, and entered their coordinates. This was done for both JK and platform. No mouse annotation tool was used. No independent human reviewer verified these labels. Estimated selection uncertainty is about 4 px; errors can be larger where front and rear frame parts are ambiguous.

What happened with the field lines?

The tested point/line models return no painted grass-line segment in the reviewed target frames at their author thresholds. PnLCalib SV does detect the JK crossbar in two frames. That is a goal-frame segment, not a painted pitch line. The line experiment therefore uses reviewed image lines.

For platform, the candidate line improves A/B consistency. For JK, adding the two assumed box lines fits those lines, but excluded corner errors remain 90, 55 and 13 px. That JK refinement is rejected for the main scene.
