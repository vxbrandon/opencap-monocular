What two-view 3D data exists?

Reviewed 5 November 2026. The older edited platform MOV produced two short conditional stereo segments. These are research estimates with unvalidated camera calibration and physical capture timing.

This page displays previously saved gravity-model fits to those stereo observations. The plotted samples are fitted positions, not independent ground-truth measurements. No new reconstruction was run for this publication.
