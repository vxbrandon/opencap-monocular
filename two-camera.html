What the synthetic run actually proves

Synthetic is the correct way to build fusion before footage exists: it is the only setting where the true answer is known, so the estimator can be scored rather than admired. These numbers are real measurements of the algorithm — they are simply not measurements of two iPhones.

Where it is weak — stated, not buried

It understates its own uncertainty. Median reported σ is 0.255 m against a median actual error of 0.453 m — a ratio of 0.564. The module is roughly 1.8× more confident than it deserves to be. Any downstream consumer that trusts σ as a gate is being lied to, gently.

Identity drifts. 22 true players produce 2.18 fused IDs each — 27 ID changes, about 1.23 per player per minute. Fine for heatmaps and team shape; not yet good enough for per-player season stats.

The ball is the worst part. Median error is a respectable 0.57 m, but RMSE is 7.99 m — the gap is false positives. Of 88 available FPs, 73 leaked through, and 14 leaked even though the other camera could see the real ball. That is the clearest thing to fix next, and it is fixable in code.

One genuine win hides in that table: when both cameras see the ball, selecting the better view gives RMSE 2.98 m versus 8.15 m for naive averaging. Averaging two ball estimates is actively harmful — a fact only the synthetic harness could have revealed.

What it does not prove

  • That two phones on a mount can be calibrated at all — untested
  • That real lens distortion, rolling shutter and IMU drift behave like the injected noise
  • That audio/IMU sync recovers the offset on real clips — the synthetic offset was recovered perfectly because it was inserted perfectly
  • That the inter-camera bias estimator sees real bias — it corrects to ~5 m synthetically and refuses beyond, but real bias has never been shown to it
  • Anything at all about the stitched-panorama video branch

The bias work carries its own caveat, and it is the important one: BIAS_MAX_CHI2 = 4.0 was derived synthetically. Before trusting any correction on real footage, it must be re-measured on a clip believed well aligned:

python scripts/t0_fuse.py --calibrate-chi2 \ --left L.jsonl --right R.jsonl --offset S

Aligned χ² ≈ 2.5 means the threshold transfers. Above 4.0 and the module refuses every correction — safe, but useless — and the ceiling has to be re-derived from that clip.
