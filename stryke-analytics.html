What it produces measured, not asserted

Coaching that names the decision

Measured

Deterministic rules — no language model — turn each reception into pressure, open space, teammate options ranked by metres gained, and what the player actually chose.

“You received in the middle third, left channel with t13 2.9 m away on your front-left, closing in 0.7 s. You played backwards to t10 (−8.7 m) — t14 was open 21.0 m on your front-right for 19.0 m of progress.”verified against the source video at three timestamps: positions, pressure side and the open teammate all real on screen

Two-camera fusion beats both cameras

Measured

A constant offset between the two independently-calibrated phones is measured and subtracted — up to 5 m in every direction tested, and refused rather than guessed beyond it, checked across 56 bias configurations. Association is sticky across time; positions are combined by expected accuracy, not averaged blindly. The ball is selected from the better camera rather than averaged — averaging puts it where it never was.

association 99.83 % fused RMSE 0.637 m vs 0.765 / 0.982 alone coverage +53 % ball RMSE 2.98 m vs 8.15 averaged bias corrected to 5 m, refused beyond

What it refuses to say the deliberate part

A measurement system earns trust by declining. Each refusal below is enforced in code and pinned by a test, not left to discipline.

Which way the team is attacking

Refuses

Five signals in three independent families vote. On the demo clip only one family could vote, giving confidence 0.23 against a 0.35 bar — so every direction-dependent claim is suppressed rather than hedged. On a second clip the old single-statistic rule would have shipped the inverted answer, telling a player he went backwards when he went forwards.

clip A conf 0.23 — right, suppressed clip B conf 0.13 — wrong, refused clip C conf 0.38 — right, hedged

Body orientation — “you received too square”

Refuses

The phrase this feature was named for is not produced. It needs pose estimation, which this pipeline does not have, so body angle, head direction and first-touch quality sit in a not_claimed list — and a test asserts they never appear in generated text.

Ball speed as a single number

Refuses

Speed scales one-for-one with depth, and depth is read from a ball-radius estimate whose published error is 24–28 %. Without a metric reference in frame the point value is withheld and only a band is shown. Launch angle is emitted as four camera solutions separated by half a pixel of fit error — where the old code picked one and printed it as fact.

band 22.9 – 48.5 km/h point value null spin: direction only, no magnitude

Coordinates from a collapsed calibration

Refuses

A homography can pass every check — enough keypoints, converged, finite — and still map the whole image onto a puddle. On amateur footage 17 players were landing inside a 0.4 × 0.2 m box while the system reported 100 % calibration. Frames whose players collapse below a 5 m spread now emit nothing.

What only you can supply 5 asks

Footage

90 seconds at a real pitch, one phone. Static, wide, slightly elevated, 4K30 at 0.5×, with the centre circle and one penalty box in frame. This measures the broadcast→phone domain shift that currently blocks the amateur path. Full recipe in CAPTURE-SPEC.md §8.

First run

One command decides whether the two-phone maths transfers to your hardware. On a clip you believe is well aligned, run t0_fuse.py --calibrate-chi2 before trusting any correction: it re-measures the two populations the safety threshold separates and prints its own go/no-go. Take that measurement deliberately on the first capture rather than inferring it later from a failure.

Rig

The two-phone capture — phones ~80° apart with overlapping view. Ultrawide is mandatory, not preference: at that spread the main lens leaves a blind gap down the middle of the pitch.

Licensing

Two emails, drafted and unsent. One to the Reco maintainer (AGPL, gates the video branch), one to the KIST authors (no licence at all, gates the code-copy route). Neither blocks current work.

Radar

A ~$300 speed gun and 100 paired kicks. Nothing else can convert the ball module from self-consistent physics into a measurement — and every accuracy claim stays unvalidated until it exists.
