The two hard gates nothing ships past these

Gate 1  Radar before rankings

Speed and placement error must be validated against a Pocket Radar (PR1000-BC, ~$300) and a physically measured goal. Until the acceptance set passes, percentiles stay unpublished and every speed on every screen is a band, never a number. Action: order the radar in week 1 — it has lead time and Stage 1 cannot start without it.

Gate 2  Licence before any build leaves the team

GVHMR is non-commercial; SMPL/SMPL-X is research-only. No external TestFlight, no investor device, no App Store submission until a Meshcapade licence is signed and GVHMR's code question is answered in writing — or the SAM-3D-Body / GEM-X migration is merged and green. Action: open the decision in week 1.
